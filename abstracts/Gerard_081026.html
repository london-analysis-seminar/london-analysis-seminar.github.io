On spectral properties of the Benjamin-Ono Lax operator

The integrability of the Benjamin-Ono equation is based on a Lax pair structure involving a self-adjoint operator on the Hardy space of the upper half plane. In this talk I will discuss the spectral properties of this operator, and how these properties can be combined with a representation formula to provide new information about long-time Benjamin-Ono dynamics. This talk is based on several joint works in collaboration with Elliot Blackstone, Louise Gassot, Jiao He and Peter Miller.
