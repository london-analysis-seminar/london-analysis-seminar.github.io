Barbara Nelli (University of L'Aquila):

Title: The Jenkins-Serrin theorem reloaded: minimal graphs in Killing submersions.

Abstract:
In the sixties, Jenkins and Serrin proved a celebrated theorem about minimal graphs in the Euclidean 3-space with infinite boundary values. After reviewing the classical theory, we show how to solve the Jenkins-Serrin problem in a 3-manifold carrying a Killing vector field. This is a joint work with A. Del Prete and J. M. Manzano.
