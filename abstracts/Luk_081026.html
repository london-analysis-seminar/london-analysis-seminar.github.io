Alexandru Aleman (Lund)

High-frequency limits for the Einstein vacuum constraint equations

Abstract: It is known in the physics literature that “high-frequency weak limits” of solutions to the Einstein vacuum equations are not necessarily vacuum solutions but may have a non-trivial stress-energy-momentum tensor, which can be interpreted physically as arising from "effective matter fields" generated by the back-reaction of high-frequency gravitational waves. The phenomenon can be studied from the point of view of initial data sets of the Einstein vacuum equations, which satisfy the Einstein vacuum constraint equations; from this perspective, it is related to questions in Riemannian geometry. I will present an ongoing joint work with Yuchen Mao, Sung-Jin Oh and Zhongkai Tao giving a complete characterization of the high-frequency limits of solutions to the Einstein vacuum constraint equations.
